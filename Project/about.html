Our story

Started with one windowsill
and too many cuttings

Terra & Leaf opened in 2014 out of a spare room, when our founder ran out of space for the plants she kept propagating. Twelve years later, we're still doing the same thing — just with a proper storefront.

How we got here

The short version of a long, plant-covered story.

2014

A spare room becomes a shop

What started as selling extra cuttings to neighbors turned into a waitlist, then a lease.

2017

We partnered with a local grower

Instead of shipping plants across the country, we started sourcing from a grower thirty minutes away.

2021

The care-tag system launched

Every plant now leaves the shop with real watering intervals, not generic care card advice.

2026

Still on the same street

Same corner, same crew, several thousand more plants sent home with happy owners.
